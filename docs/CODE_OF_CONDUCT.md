# Code of Conduct

**Maintainer:** LaniakeA Dev

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the LaniakeA Protocol community a
respectful and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be welcoming and patient.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to `dev@laniakea-protocol.org`. The LaniakeA Dev
team will review and respond.
